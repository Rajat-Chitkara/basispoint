About

An independent research practice

The Basis Point publishes free research on Indian markets and takes on commissioned work to the same standard. Both are built the same way: from primary documents, with the assumptions left visible.

Why this exists

Most market numbers in circulation are inherited rather than measured. A figure appears in a deck, gets cited in a press release, is picked up by a database, and within two years it is "the market size" — with no one able to say which document it came from or what was counted.

The Basis Point works the other way round. A question is answered by going to the documents that are legally required to be filed, reading them scheme by scheme or company by company, and adding up what is actually there. It is slower. It produces numbers that survive being questioned.

What independent means here

Specifically, and in a way you can check:

  • No vendor funding. Published reports are not sponsored, syndicated, or paid for by any party with an interest in the result.
  • No syndicated data. Figures come from filings, regulatory disclosures and audited accounts — not from purchased market-research databases whose own sourcing cannot be inspected.
  • Sources named. Every material figure in a report is attributed to a document you can retrieve yourself.
  • Commissioned work is separated from published work. A client cannot buy a conclusion in a published report, and published research is never used as a sales instrument for a client's position.

Free is the point. Every issue is free and always will be. The published research exists to show the method in full, on real questions, at full length — so that anyone considering commissioning work can judge the standard before paying for anything.

Who runs it

The Basis Point is run by Rajat Chitkara, who has three years of experience in market research.

It exists for a straightforward reason: to publish good independent research on topics worth understanding properly, without waiting for someone to commission it first.

Work is delivered by a single analyst. That is a deliberate constraint: it caps how many engagements run at once, and it means the person you speak to is the person reading the filings.

How the two halves fit together

The published reports are the portfolio. The commissioned services are the practice. A prospective client can read a full report end to end, check the numbers against the sources, and decide from that whether the method suits the decision they are trying to make.

If you want to see how a report is put together before commissioning anything, the methodology page sets it out step by step.
